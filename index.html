01 / About

The short version of a longer story.

Why do some posts stop the scroll and most don't? That question became a habit.

Chasing that answer turned into testing hooks, studying what makes people comment instead of just double-tapping, and slowly turning "I think this works" into "I can prove this works."

That habit paid off two ways. As a Sales Executive at Tech Cuber, I read what a client actually needed and closed the gap between "interested" and "signed." As a marketer for The Virtual Advertisers and The Virtual Academy, I did the same for entire brands.

The Computer Science background is the long game. I'm now pursuing a Generative AI diploma at NED Academy, because I want to stop asking a developer for a landing page and start being the person who builds it.

Day to day, that means splitting my time between actual sales conversations and the content that leads into them, treating both as one connected job instead of two separate ones. If you're curious why I think that split hurts most brands, I wrote a longer breakdown of it on the blog.
